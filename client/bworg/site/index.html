What Bison unlocks

Trade without giving up custody

Swap directly from your wallet using atomic swaps. No accounts. No custodians. No per-trade platform fees. You pay normal network fees. To enable trading, post a small time-locked, refundable DCR bond that deters spam.

Privacy on your terms

Turn on mixing for Decred when you want it. Keep an audit trail when you need it. Privacy lives in the wallet, under your control.

Start fast, stay light

Sync in minutes with SPV. No full‑chain downloads. Great on laptops and workstations.

Automate liquidity (optional)

Agent‑ready market‑maker bots can provide liquidity on the built‑in DEX and help you capture spreads. You choose when they run and what they can do.

Self-custody, always

You hold the keys. Only you can move your coins. No sign‑ups. No withdrawal holds. No middlemen.

FAQ's

Do I need an account to trade?

No. Trades are wallet-to-wallet via atomic swaps. No custodians and no exchange accounts.

Do I need an account to trade?

No. Trades are wallet-to-wallet via atomic swaps. No custodians and no exchange accounts.

How fast is setup?

Bison uses SPV so you can get going in minutes rather than downloading full chains.

What are the fees?

The DEX charges no trading fees. You pay normal on‑chain network fees. To enable trading, you post a refundable DCR bond that prevents spam.

Can I automate market making?

Yes. Optional market‑maker bots can provide liquidity on the DEX and help capture spreads. You control when and how they run.
